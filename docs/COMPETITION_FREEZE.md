# Amazon submission freeze

The organizer's October 19, 2026 reminder requires the submitted repository, video and other project materials to remain unchanged after the deadline until after winners are announced. Even minor edits may affect eligibility. This applies to the whole submitted repository, not just its main branch or a tag.

- Submission deadline: **October 23, 2026, 12:00 PDT** = **October 24, 2026, 03:00 Asia/Taipei** = **October 23, 2026, 19:00 UTC**.
- Planned winner announcement: **on or around December 3, 2026, 12:00 PST** = **December 4, 2026, 04:00 Asia/Taipei**.
- Release the freeze only after the actual organizer announcement has been verified. A calendar date alone is insufficient.
- Authority: [official rules](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules), plus the stricter direct organizer reminder. The final submission inventory records the exact URLs and source version.

During the freeze, do not push commits or branches, alter tags/releases/assets, revise repository descriptions or documentation, update video metadata/captions/thumbnail/visibility, change the Devpost entry/gallery, or modify linked demonstrations and downloads. Read-only review is allowed. Keep future development in a separate private repository that is not part of the submitted materials. No automatic deployment is configured; do not enable dependency-update or publishing automation on the frozen entry.

If a security, privacy or rights issue requires a change, preserve the evidence and seek the organizer's explicit allowance before altering a submitted artifact. Do not silently patch or roll back the entry during judging.
